Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 21, 2026

Last Updated: March 21, 2026

1. Introduction

LatticeVeil is an independently developed game project committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Game and related services.

We are committed to transparency and minimal data collection. This policy is designed to be clear, comprehensive, and easy to understand.

2. Information We Collect

LatticeVeil is designed to collect minimal personal information. We believe in privacy by design and only collect what is necessary to provide and improve our services.

🔍 Information We DO NOT Collect

  • No location tracking - We do not track, store, or access your physical location
  • No personal identifiers - We do not collect your name, address, phone number, or other personal contact information
  • No usage analytics - We do not track how you play, what features you use, or your gameplay patterns
  • No device fingerprinting - We do not create profiles based on your device characteristics
  • No marketing data - We do not collect information for advertising or marketing purposes
  • No social media integration - We do not automatically access or collect your social media profiles

📊 Information We MAY Collect

  • Account Information - If you choose to create an account, we only store your email address and username
  • Game Progress - Local save files stored on your device (we do not access or upload these)
  • Technical Information - Basic device information needed to ensure compatibility and performance
  • Supporter Information - PayPal transaction data if you choose to support development (processed by PayPal, not us)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for the following purposes:

🎮 Core Game Functionality

  • Account management and authentication
  • Providing technical support when you contact us
  • Ensuring game compatibility and performance

🔧 Service Improvement

  • Fixing bugs and technical issues
  • Adding new features based on user feedback
  • Improving game performance and stability

📧 Legal Compliance

  • Responding to legal requests and inquiries
  • Complying with applicable laws and regulations
  • Protecting our rights and intellectual property

4. Online Multiplayer & Third-Party Services

LatticeVeil may include online multiplayer features powered by Epic Online Services (EOS). These services have their own privacy practices:

🌐 Epic Online Services

When you use online features, certain data is handled by Epic Games according to their own policies:

  • Authentication: Epic Games handles account authentication and login
  • Networking: Online gameplay data is transmitted through Epic's infrastructure
  • Matchmaking: Player matching and session management

📋 Epic Games Privacy Policy

For comprehensive information about how Epic Games handles online multiplayer data, please review their official policies:

  • Epic Games Terms of Service
  • Epic Games Privacy Policy
  • EOS Developer Documentation
  • EOS Privacy Support

📺 Future Advertisement Privacy

If we integrate advertisements in the future, our privacy approach will remain:

  • Minimal data collection - Ads will use the minimum data necessary for functionality
  • Family-friendly only - All advertisements will be appropriate for all ages
  • User control - You will have control over ad preferences and data usage
  • Opt-out options - Supporters may receive ad-free experience
  • Third-party compliance - Ad partners will be vetted for privacy compliance
  • No behavioral tracking - We will not track your behavior across websites or apps

🎁 Starter Pack Data Handling

Future starter pack features will handle data minimally:

  • Local preference storage - Starter pack choices stored locally on your device
  • No personal data collection - Starter selections do not require personal information
  • Game save integration - Starter choices saved with your game progress
  • One-time selection - Choices are permanent per save to prevent abuse
  • No analytics tracking - We will not track which starter options players choose

🔄 Launcher Update Data Practices

LatticeVeil uses an automatic launcher update system that handles data with privacy in mind:

  • Version information only - Only version numbers and update metadata are transmitted
  • No personal data collection - Update checks do not collect personal information
  • Local version storage - Current version information stored locally on your device
  • Secure download channels - Updates only downloaded from official GitHub releases
  • Backup data handling - Update backups stored locally and never transmitted
  • No usage analytics - We do not track update frequency or user behavior
  • Hash verification - Game executable hashes verified for security and integrity
  • Delta update efficiency - Only changed files downloaded to minimize data transfer

🔐 Hash Verification & Security

To ensure game integrity and fair play, LatticeVeil uses hash verification:

  • Executable Hashing - Game EXE files are verified against authorized hashes
  • No Personal Data - Hash verification does not collect or transmit personal information
  • Security Only - Hashes used solely for integrity verification and anti-cheat protection
  • Local Verification - Hash calculations performed locally on your device
  • Modified Builds - Modified game versions can run locally but cannot access online features

🛡️ Security Measures

  • Encryption: Account data is transmitted using secure encryption protocols
  • Access Controls: We limit access to your personal information to authorized personnel only
  • Regular Updates: We maintain and update our security practices regularly

7. Cookies & Local Storage

LatticeVeil uses minimal tracking technologies:

🍪 Cookies
